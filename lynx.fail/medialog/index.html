Yu-Gi-Oh! (1998)

2/13/26 - tbd
3/27 episodes
★★★★★

thoughts ahh, season 0.. my first foray into the yu-gi-oh franchise. i'd say it's a good series to start out on, even though it's a bit of different tone than the rest of the franchise! not only does it give a bit of backstory into yugi and jonouchi's friendship (and honda too i guess), which duel monsters only shows brief flashbacks of, it also shows yu-gi-oh's darker origins and a bit of how it was originally intended to be a horror series. i ADORE the art style of this season, and also yami being shown as this shadowy entity.
as this was my first intro to ygo, i actually got used to green-haired kaiba LMAO. i was a bit surprised to learn he had brown hair in every other iteration! it's nice to be able to revisit this series, i think this will be my third? time watching season 0!
