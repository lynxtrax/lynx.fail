    PLURRH

    peace, love, unity, respect, responsibility, and hope!
    this movement has its roots in rave and EDM culture, but i think it's something everybody should strive to follow ♡ i aspire to live with plurrh in my heart. i really want to leave a positive mark on the people's lives that i'm in and hopefully inspire them to do the same to others.
