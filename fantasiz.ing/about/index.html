ROAR i'm listening to ROAR right now as i create this page and god i love his music so much. he's simultaneously known yet not. and i'm so grateful for I Can't Handle Change / I'm Not Here To Make Friends' popularity but i also wish i could find people who have listened to something other than a song off those EPs. Diamond Destroyer of Death is my favorite album of his but honestly his whole discography is GREAT. If I had to say I don't think Knives for Aries is the best, but it certaintly grew on me and it's super catchy. but AGHHHH like Wondering Why is so good. so is Explosion of Birds. and Fading Kitten Syndrome actually made me sob the first time I listened to it, i had to avoid it for a while because it'd make me cry LMAO. it's my dream to see him on tour sometime. *shakes you* listen to ROAR. for me please. PLEASEkjrf. i also saw HKFY and the lead singer recognized my ROAR shirt and that was the best thing ever. that's all.
sixwing i never rlly listened salvia hunden's music as MAILPUP despite being into those genres but i did give her new album, my teeth will free you, a try and oh my god is it beautiful. the songs are so artistic and unique. my favorite would have to be seraph or dogwhistle. this album is definitely one to sit down and listen to! 3/31 really got me in the feels. if the themes of the album are something you can relate to, it really gets to you.
Pierce The Veil the first emo band i really got into in my journey as a young wannabe scene kid. their music really means a lot to me and has got me through some tough times. i saw them once with my best friend and it was soo much fun and i'm seeing them again on the 19th and i can't WAIT!! genuinely all their songs are so good. my favorite album is probably their first one, A Flair For The Dramatic. it was so cool being there for the release of The Jaws of Life and just seeing how their music has evolved throughout the years.
