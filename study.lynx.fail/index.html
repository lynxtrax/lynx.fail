preface

07/21/26

as i've graduated highschool, i feel like my intelligence has declined. the brain is a muscle, right? i haven't been exercising it at all! i feel as if i don't grasp things as well as i used to, my vocabulary has shrinked, i just... feel so out of the loop. and i'm tired of it.

i have a very bad memory. i don't want to learn, just for it to be in vain because i forgot about it however many weeks later. i also feel like i have trouble internalizing new ideas if all i do is read/watch/whatever. i need to apply it in some shape or form.

that's where this website comes in! i wanted to be able to write and organize all my thoughts out as i read, watch, and learn different things. i like to use html to write stuff out and i thought making a mini site where i just document what i learn could be neat. and i'm making it public just for the fun of it ;p
